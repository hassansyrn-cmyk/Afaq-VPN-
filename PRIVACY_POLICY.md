# Afaq VPN Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

This MVP does not sell personal data and does not intentionally collect or retain browsing history, DNS queries, traffic content, or destination logs. It contains no analytics, advertising, Firebase, or crash-reporting SDK.

The app processes the WireGuard configuration on the device to establish the VPN tunnel. The selected VPN server necessarily handles encrypted tunnel packets and forwards network traffic; the server operator must maintain a separate, accurate server privacy policy and secure operational controls. The included server scripts do not add traffic logging.

Settings such as language, theme, consent, and connection preferences are stored locally. A client private key may be supplied locally for testing and must never be committed. A production release should generate it on-device and protect it with Android Keystore-backed storage.

If crash reporting is added later, this policy and the in-app disclosure must be updated before release, describing the exact minimum diagnostic data collected and retention period.
