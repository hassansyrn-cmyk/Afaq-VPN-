## English

### 1. Introduction

Afaq VPN is operated by Hasan Hussein, based in the United Arab Emirates.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Afaq VPN processes, why it is processed, how it is protected, and the choices available to users.

By using Afaq VPN, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

### 2. Information We Process

Afaq VPN does not require users to create an account or provide a name, phone number, or postal address.

The app and its supporting services may process the following limited information:

#### VPN provisioning and security information

To register a device and create a WireGuard VPN configuration, Afaq VPN may process:

- A device-generated identifier used by Afaq VPN.
- The WireGuard public key generated for the VPN connection.
- The IP address from which a registration or security request is received.
- VPN client status, assigned internal VPN address, and registration status.
- Request timestamps and limited security records used to prevent replay attacks, duplicate registrations, automated abuse, and unauthorized access.

The WireGuard private key should remain on the user’s device and is not intended to be transmitted to Afaq VPN servers.

#### Google Play Integrity

Afaq VPN uses Google Play Integrity to help verify that requests originate from a genuine installation of the app running on an eligible Android device.

Google Play Integrity may process device, application, licensing, and integrity-related signals according to Google’s own privacy practices. Afaq VPN receives and processes the integrity result and related request-validation information for security and fraud-prevention purposes.

#### Advertising information

Afaq VPN uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

- Advertising identifiers.
- Device and application information.
- IP address.
- General location derived from the IP address.
- Advertisement interactions and performance information.
- Diagnostic and fraud-prevention information.

The exact information processed by Google AdMob may depend on the user’s device, region, privacy choices, consent status, and the type of advertisements displayed.

Where required by applicable law, the app will request the user’s consent before serving personalized advertisements. Users who do not consent may receive non-personalized or limited advertisements, where available.

Google processes advertising information according to its own privacy policies and advertising requirements.

### 3. VPN Traffic and Browsing Activity

Afaq VPN does not intentionally collect, sell, or store:

- Browsing history.
- DNS query history.
- The content of internet communications.
- Passwords or messages transmitted through the VPN.
- A list of websites or online services visited by the user.
- The content of encrypted VPN traffic.

The selected VPN server necessarily receives encrypted WireGuard tunnel packets and forwards network traffic to provide the VPN service.

Afaq VPN’s server configuration is not intended to create browsing-history, DNS-history, traffic-content, or destination logs. However, limited technical and security information may be processed temporarily by hosting providers, network infrastructure, operating systems, or security services where necessary to operate and protect the service.

Afaq VPN does not guarantee anonymity and cannot control information independently collected by websites, internet service providers outside the VPN tunnel, advertising providers, device manufacturers, operating-system providers, or third-party services used by the user.

### 4. Locally Stored Information

Some settings may be stored locally on the user’s device, including:

- Language and appearance preferences.
- Consent and advertising choices.
- Connection preferences.
- VPN configuration and connection status.
- Device-generated cryptographic material required for WireGuard.

Locally stored information remains on the device unless it is included in a device backup, removed by the operating system, or deleted when the app’s data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

### 5. Purposes of Processing

The limited information described in this policy may be processed to:

- Establish and maintain the VPN connection.
- Generate and manage WireGuard client configurations.
- Verify app and device integrity.
- Prevent replay attacks, fraud, unauthorized access, and automated abuse.
- Enforce registration and request limits.
- Maintain service security, stability, and availability.
- Display and measure advertisements.
- Diagnose technical problems.
- Comply with legal obligations and valid legal requests.

### 6. Data Sharing

Afaq VPN does not sell users’ personal information.

Limited information may be processed by or shared with service providers when necessary to provide and protect the app, including:

- Google Play Integrity, for application and device integrity verification.
- Google AdMob and its advertising partners, for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.
- VPN server and infrastructure providers, for hosting and network connectivity.
- Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or to protect users, the service, or the public.

These third parties may process information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

### 7. Data Retention

Active WireGuard provisioning information, including the public key, assigned internal VPN address, device registration identifier, and client status, may be retained for as long as the device remains registered or the VPN service remains active.

Security, rate-limiting, request-validation, and anti-abuse records are intended to be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for security and service protection, normally for up to 90 days, unless a longer period is required to investigate abuse, resolve a technical or legal issue, comply with law, or protect the service.

Information processed by Google AdMob, Google Play Integrity, hosting providers, or other third parties is retained according to the applicable provider’s policies and settings.

### 8. Data Security

Afaq VPN uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including encrypted network communications, access controls, request verification, request limits, anti-replay protections, and restricted server access.

WireGuard uses encrypted tunnels to protect traffic between the user’s device and the selected VPN server.

No system, network, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for keeping their devices updated and protected from unauthorized access.

### 9. Data Deletion and User Requests

Afaq VPN does not require an account. Therefore, there is no in-app user account to delete.

Users may request deletion of VPN registration and provisioning information associated with their device by contacting:

**hassan.syrn@gmail.com**

A deletion request should include enough non-sensitive information to identify the relevant VPN registration. Users should never send a WireGuard private key, password, or other secret credential by email.

Some information may be retained when reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, investigate abuse, protect the service, or resolve security incidents.

Users can also remove locally stored app information by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling Afaq VPN. This does not automatically delete information previously received by Afaq VPN servers or third-party service providers.

### 10. Children’s Privacy

Afaq VPN is not designed for or directed to children under the age of 13.

Afaq VPN does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If the developer becomes aware that information has been collected from a child under 13, reasonable steps will be taken to delete it.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided information through Afaq VPN may contact the developer at the email address below.

### 11. International Processing

Afaq VPN is operated from the United Arab Emirates. Information may be processed in the United Arab Emirates or in other countries where VPN infrastructure, Google services, advertising providers, or hosting providers operate.

These countries may have data-protection laws different from those in the user’s country.

### 12. Third-Party Services

Afaq VPN may use third-party services governed by their own terms and privacy policies, including:

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, advertising configuration, VPN infrastructure, legal requirements, or data-processing practices change.

The updated version will be published at the official Privacy Policy URL with a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated within the app where appropriate.

#### معلومات تسجيل VPN والحماية

لتسجيل الجهاز وإنشاء إعداد اتصال WireGuard، قد يعالج Afaq VPN ما يلي:

- معرّف يتم إنشاؤه للجهاز ويستخدمه تطبيق Afaq VPN.

يجب أن يبقى المفتاح الخاص لـ WireGuard على جهاز المستخدم، وليس من المفترض إرساله إلى خوادم Afaq VPN.

#### Google Play Integrity

يستخدم Afaq VPN خدمة Google Play Integrity للمساعدة في التحقق من أن الطلبات صادرة عن نسخة أصلية من التطبيق تعمل على جهاز Android مؤهل.

- معرّفات الإعلانات.
- معلومات الجهاز والتطبيق.
- عنوان IP.
- الموقع العام المستنتج من عنوان IP.
